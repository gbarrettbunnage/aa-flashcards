# AA Skills Builders M3



When you are asked to evaluate acceptance/continuance risks:

You need to identify facts from the scenario to consider how they impact the audit firm’s risks.



Then, as a part of the analysis, you should explain the importance of the facts in relation to the acceptance decision. 



Use the seven procedures as a checklist to identify all of the issues relevant to the question. 



If you are then asked for a response to those risks that would enable the firm to take on the client:

You must ensure that you can identify and explain how these acceptance risks impact the audit firm and be able to propose safeguards, where appropriate, to mitigate each risk or reduce it to an acceptable level.



You must also be able to identify when an auditor is unable to propose sufficient safeguards or is prohibited outright from undertaking the engagement.



#### Question 1



SCENARIO



You are the audit manager on the audit of the financial statements of Gelding Pharmaceuticals plc (‘GP’) and have completed the audit for the year ended 30 April 20X1. GP is a pharmaceutical research, development, production and testing company whose customers comprise both development centres such as universities and international distributors. The company operates from its headquarters in Sheffield but attracts worldwide clients. GP has research hubs in various locations throughout the world which are focused on sourcing ingredients for future developments. The company is fully licensed and has been operating successfully for 12 years. The company was listed on the London Stock Exchange on 1 May 20X1.



Sally Breeder, the audit partner, has mentioned that the schedule of work for the audit to the year ended 30 April 20X2 will change as a result of GP’s listing, with more work to be carried out throughout the financial year. She has asked you to prepare notes for inclusion in the audit file evaluating any continuance risks that require consideration in respect of the firm’s planned continuance as auditor of GP. She has asked you to refer to her notes from the meeting with James and Edward for any points requiring consideration.



Consider GP to be a public interest entity for the purposes of this question



APPENDIX - Notes from meeting with James Foal (GP’s deputy finance director) and Edward Johnson (operations director) held on 3 August 20X1.



Both James and Edward thanked us for the audit team’s hard work in getting the audit to this point and indicated how beneficial it was that the audit firm has significant experience in dealing with listed companies, meaning that they had not had to consider changing audit firms following the listing of GP in May. They were also delighted for Sally to confirm that Anna Wallington from the audit firm would be joining GP on a one-month secondment in August to manage GP’s internal audit department while they wait for the new head of internal audit to start in September following the sudden death of the longstanding former team leader.



Changes to GP’s board of directors - The relevant additions to the board are in place following the listing of the company, with a full contingent of non-executive directors. Edward mentioned that the new Chair has stated that she is looking forward to catching up with Sally again to discuss their golf club committee. She also mentioned that she was looking forward to Sally briefing her on some of the historical company information since she has been the audit partner for the last seven years.



Research and development (R\&D) - James requested the audit firm help with the review and resolution of an ongoing dispute with HMRC regarding an R\&D tax claim. The claim is for a substantial amount and has been refused by HMRC on the basis that it doesn’t qualify for the relief.



Change of direction - Edward advised you of GP’s intention to move into the developing area of cloning and genetic manipulation, with a particular focus on chromosomal selection in IVF treatment. He is aware that this is a very expensive specific area of research and does not know how it will affect financial statements at this point. He stated that this area has critics within the media and society at large. There have been indications that there will be protests at the company’s headquarters due to this controversial change in direction, but that it is GP’s intention to go ahead as this may be highly profitable. Edward noted that competitors are thinking of similar research, and he would like GP to be at the forefront of this biological revolution.



Undisclosed location - Edward made you aware of one of the projects that he expects to feature significantly in the coming year’s financial statements. One of the research hubs in South America is currently working on a vaccine for a highly contagious illness. This is a secret project that requires a substantial amount of funding from GP. Edward made it clear that he was not prepared to divulge any details about the location of this project, but that he anticipates that it will be a substantial element of the research activities of GP for up to two years. He stated it will only be on completion of the project that any of the details, including financials, will be divulged to those outside the board and this secrecy is not open for negotiation.
